# Creator Fund

The [Creator Fund](https://decentraland.org/creator-fund) rewards creators whose Decentraland experiences attract and keep real players. Each Cycle, a fixed pool is shared among Qualifying Experiences based on the engagement they generate, and Rewards are paid in USDC within 14 days after the Cycle ends.

This page is part of the [**Creator Fund Program Terms**](https://decentraland.org/creator-fund-terms). If anything here conflicts with the Program Terms, the Program Terms prevail. Capitalized terms such as Cycle, Reward, Owner Wallet, Payout Wallet, Qualifying Experience and Qualifying Player have the meaning given in the Program Terms. We update this page as the Program evolves; every change is listed at the bottom.

These rules apply to the **Beta Season**. All dates and times on this page are in UTC.

## Who can take part

To enroll, you must meet all the eligibility requirements in [Section 3 of the Program Terms](https://decentraland.org/creator-fund-terms#eligibility). In short, you must:

* be at least 18;
* own the NAME or World where your experience is deployed; and
* opt in through [Creator Hub](https://decentraland.org/download/creator-hub) by signing the Program Terms with that wallet.

Opt-in opens in Creator Hub on November 11, 2026.

People who run the Program, such as DAO Council members and Foundation or Regenesis Labs contractors, can't take part, with some exceptions set out in the Terms.

One opt-in covers every eligible NAME and World owned by your Owner Wallet, so you don't need a separate opt-in for each. Scenes you publish later join when you next open the Creator Fund tab in Creator Hub.

Rewards are paid to the Payout Wallet you set in Creator Hub. If you haven't set one 7 days after a Cycle ends, the Reward payout is lost.

### Joining mid-Cycle and transfers

If you join during a Cycle and have owned your NAME or World since it started, the whole Cycle counts. If the NAME or World was transferred to you during the Cycle, you count from the next one.

If you transfer a NAME or World during a Cycle, its experience stops counting from the transfer, and its activity in that Cycle doesn't count toward anyone's Reward. Rewards for Cycles that ended before the transfer are still paid to the wallet that owned it when each of those Cycles ended.

## How it works: what we measure

The goal is to reward the value your experience brings to Decentraland. Each Cycle Allocation is shared proportionally among Qualifying Experiences based on engagement from Qualifying Players, measured with these metrics:

### Active Playtime

Time players actively spend in your experience.

### Player Retention

Players who come back to your experience on the days after playing it.

### New Decentraland Players

Players who start in Decentraland by coming to your experience. Players acquired through Foundation advertising campaigns don't count.

These metrics may change during the Program. If we add, remove or redefine a metric, we update this page before the Cycle in which the change applies.

## What counts

**Your experience counts** in a Cycle if, for the whole Cycle, it complies with the Decentraland [Terms of Use](https://decentraland.org/terms/) and [Content Policy](https://decentraland.org/content/) and has no gambling or other 18+ restricted content. Scenes in Genesis City and public Worlds count; a World that needs a password, an invitation or an NFT to enter doesn't. An experience that is removed, made private or transferred stops counting from that moment.

**Qualifying Players.** Only real people playing on the official Decentraland desktop or mobile app, in line with the Terms of Use, count. These never count:

* sessions by you, your collaborators or other Program participants;
* sessions by people who run the Program;
* visits rewarded by a Rewards Program season running at the same time.

## Payout process

In the Beta Season, Rewards are paid as follows:

* **When:** within 14 days after each Cycle ends.
* **Currency:** USDC (ERC-20) on Ethereum mainnet.
* **Paid to:** the Payout Wallet you set in Creator Hub. If you haven't set one 7 days after a Cycle ends, the Reward payout is lost.
* **Minimum Reward:** USD 5 per Cycle. Lower amounts are not paid and are not carried forward.
* **Network fee:** paid by the Decentraland Foundation and Regenesis Labs.
* **Sanctions check:** every Payout Wallet may be checked against public sanctions lists before payment.

Other payment options, such as MANA, may be offered in later Seasons.

### Set up your Payout Wallet

Set your Payout Wallet in Creator Hub. You'll need to confirm your email and confirm that you have access to the wallet or exchange account you add. If you haven't set one 7 days after a Cycle ends, the Reward payout is lost. The [Season Pool table](#how-it-works-the-season-pool) gives the date for each Cycle.

## Your dashboard and results

Creator Hub shows your experience's metrics during the Cycle so you can follow how it is doing. These figures are for information only: Rewards are calculated after our review and further bot filtering, so the numbers used for your Reward may differ from your dashboard.

After each Cycle we publish a public leaderboard of the top Qualifying Experiences and the Reward each one received. Your detailed figures, your total payout across all your experiences and the wallet paid are shown only to you, in the Creator Fund Payouts section of Creator Hub.

## Fair play

We check every Cycle for bots and artificial activity and filter it out. We run automated and manual checks to make sure Rewards reflect genuine player engagement. We don't publish how these checks work.

If we find manipulation, we may disregard the affected activity, withhold Rewards and exclude you from the Program, as set out in the [Program Terms](https://decentraland.org/creator-fund-terms#fair-play-and-exclusion). If you didn't cause the activity, we only disregard it; otherwise the exclusion is permanent. Every exclusion is reviewed by a person before it takes effect, and you can send us information before the decision is final.

## Changes to these rules

We may update this page, for example to publish new Cycle Allocations or adjust the metrics. Changes apply from the Cycle stated and never affect Rewards already calculated. Changes to the [Program Terms](https://decentraland.org/creator-fund-terms#changes-suspension-and-termination) themselves follow the notice process in those Terms.
