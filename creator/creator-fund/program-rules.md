# Creator Fund Program Rules

The Creator Fund rewards creators whose Decentraland experiences attract and keep real players. Each Cycle, a fixed pool is shared among qualifying experiences based on the engagement they generate, and Rewards are paid in USDC within 14 days after the Cycle ends.

This page is part of the **Creator Fund Program Terms**. If anything here conflicts with the Program Terms, the Program Terms prevail. We update this page as the Program evolves; every change is listed at the bottom.

These rules apply to the **Beta Season**.

## Who can take part

To enroll, you must meet all the eligibility requirements in Section 3 of the Program Terms. In short, you must:

* be at least 18;
* own the NAME or World where your experience is deployed; and
* opt in through [Creator Hub](https://decentraland.org/download/creator-hub) by signing the Program Terms with that wallet.

People who run the Program, such as DAO Council members and Foundation or Regenesis Labs contractors, can't take part, with some exceptions set out in the Terms.

One opt-in covers every eligible NAME and World owned by your Owner Wallet, so you don't need a separate opt-in for each. Scenes you publish later join when you next open the Creator Fund tab in Creator Hub.

Rewards are paid to the Payout Wallet you set in Creator Hub. If you haven't set one 7 days after a Cycle ends, the Reward payout is lost.

### Joining mid-Cycle

If you join during a Cycle and have owned your NAME or World since it started, the whole Cycle counts. If you acquired it during the Cycle through a scene transfer, you count from the next one.

## How it works: the Season Pool

Each Season has a fixed amount available for Rewards, split across its Cycles. The allocation for each Cycle is published here before the Cycle starts.

**Beta Season:** November 15 to December 31, 2026 · Cycles of two weeks · Season Pool USD 30,000

| Cycle   | Dates                  | Cycle Allocation | Payment by        |
| ------- | ---------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Cycle 1 | November 15–30, 2026   | USD 10,000       | December 10, 2026 |
| Cycle 2 | December 1–15, 2026    | USD 10,000       | December 23, 2026 |
| Cycle 3 | December 16–31, 2026   | USD 10,000       | January 11, 2027  |

We may increase a Cycle Allocation at any time. We may reduce a given Cycle's allocation only if it has not yet started, with at least 7 days' notice.

## How it works: what we measure

The goal is to reward the value your experience brings to Decentraland. Each Cycle Allocation is shared proportionally among qualifying experiences based on engagement from Qualifying Players, measured with these metrics:

### Active Playtime

Time players actively spend in your experience.

### Player Retention

Players who come back to your experience on the days after playing it.

### New Decentraland Players

Players who start in Decentraland by coming to your experience. Players acquired through Foundation advertising campaigns don't count.

These metrics may change during the Program. If we add, remove or redefine a metric, we update this page before the Cycle in which the change applies.

## What counts

**Your experience counts** in a Cycle if, for the whole Cycle, it complies with the Decentraland [Terms of Use](https://decentraland.org/terms/) and [Content Policy](https://decentraland.org/content/) and has no gambling or other 18+ restricted content. An experience that is removed or transferred stops counting from that moment.

**Real players, real play.** Only real players using the Decentraland desktop or mobile app count.

We check every Cycle for bots and artificial activity, and we have mechanisms in place to filter it out. Creators who inflate their numbers unfairly may be disqualified from the Fund.

## Payout process

In the Beta Season, Rewards are paid as follows:

* **When:** within 14 days after each Cycle ends.
* **Currency:** USDC (ERC-20) on Ethereum mainnet.
* **Paid to:** the Payout Wallet you set in Creator Hub. If you haven't set one 7 days after a Cycle ends, the Reward payout is lost.
* **Minimum Reward:** USD 5 per Cycle. Lower amounts are not paid and are not carried forward.
* **Network fee:** paid by the Decentraland Foundation and Regenesis Labs.
* **Sanctions check:** every Payout Wallet may be checked against public sanctions lists before payment.

Other payment options, such as MANA, may be offered in later Seasons.

### Set up your Payout Wallet

You can set your Payout Wallet in Creator Hub before the first payment. You'll need to confirm your email and confirm that you have access to the wallet or exchange account you add. If you haven't set one 7 days after a Cycle ends, the Reward payout is lost.

## Your dashboard and results

Creator Hub shows your experience's metrics during the Cycle so you can follow how it is doing. These figures are for information only: Rewards are calculated after our review and further bot filtering, so the numbers used for your Reward may differ from your dashboard.

After each Cycle we may publish a ranking of Qualifying Experiences by amount awarded. Your detailed figures, global payout and the wallet paid are shown only to you, in the Creator Fund Payouts section of Creator Hub.

## Real players

We run automated and manual checks to make sure Rewards reflect genuine player engagement. We don't publish how these checks work.

If we find manipulation, we may disregard the affected activity, withhold Rewards, and exclude you from the Program for one or more Seasons, as set out in the Program Terms. Every exclusion is reviewed by a person before it takes effect, and you can send us information before the decision is final.

## Changes to these rules

We may update this page between Cycles, for example to publish new Cycle Allocations or adjust the metrics. Changes apply from the Cycle stated and never affect Rewards already calculated. Changes to the Program Terms themselves follow the notice process in those Terms.
