# Fastlane Bowling

- [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/stom66/dcl-bowling)
- [Play on Decentraland](https://decentraland.org/jump?realm=stom.dcl.eth)

Study a multiplayer bowling game that simulates ball and pin physics on the Multiplayer Server. Clients receive and replay compressed physics keyframes, ensuring every player sees the same roll. The game also persists player progression and uses multiple leaderboards and storage-backed components.
