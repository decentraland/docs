# Selling in the Shop

The [**Decentraland Shop**](https://decentraland.org/shop) is where players buy wearables, emotes and NAMEs. Besides MANA, items in the Shop can be priced in **Credits**, the Shop's own currency.

This page explains how to price your items in Credits, how to follow your sales in **My Store**, and how to put your collections on sale with **discounts**.

## **Pricing in Credits**

One Credit is worth **0.10 USD**, so an item priced at 50 Credits costs 5 USD. Unlike a price in MANA, a price in Credits does not move with the MANA exchange rate.

Items you listed in MANA keep working as before. The Shop calls them **classic pricing**. To move them to Credits, go to **Activity** in the Shop and use **Bring your listings into the new shop** to move them all at once, or switch them one by one from **Activity → Listings**. The Shop suggests a price in Credits based on the current MANA exchange rate, and you can adjust it before publishing.

> Discounts only apply to items priced in Credits. Items with classic pricing keep their price while a discount runs.

## **My Store**

If you have published collections, **My Store** shows how they are doing. Once you are signed in, it appears in the Shop's top navigation bar. It has four tabs:

* **Overview**: items sold, earnings, listed items, royalties from resales and discounts running, for the last 7 days, the last 30 days or all time.
* **Collections**: each of your collections with its sales, earnings and stock.
* **Discounts**: every discount you created, running ones first.
* **Audience**: who buys your items.

## **Discounts**

A discount lowers the price of every item in a collection that is listed in Credits, for a time you choose. When it ends, prices go back to normal by themselves.

### **Creating a discount**

1. Go to **My Store** and click **Create a discount**, in the **Collections** tab or in the **Discounts** tab.
2. Pick the collection you want to put on sale.
3. Pick a discount: **10%**, **20%**, **30%** or **50%**, or choose **Custom** to set any whole number between **5%** and **70%**.
4. Choose when it runs: start it **now**, or schedule it to start **on a date**. A discount can run for **up to 30 days**.
5. Optionally, limit how many **copies** can sell at the discounted price. Leave it empty to discount every listed copy.
6. Review the discount and confirm it with your wallet.

The review step lists which items get the discount. Items that are not listed, or that still use classic pricing, keep their price.

### **How prices are rounded**

Prices in Credits are whole numbers, so the discounted price is rounded up to the next whole Credit, and it is always at least 1 Credit. For example, a 50% discount on an item priced at 25 Credits sells it for 13 Credits.

On very cheap items a small discount may not change the price at all. The form warns you when that happens, so you can pick a bigger discount.

### **Ending a discount early**

A discount ends on its own when its time is up, or when all the copies you allowed have sold at the discounted price. To stop it sooner, open the **Discounts** tab in My Store and click **End discount** on it.

### **What buyers see**

While a discount runs, your items show the discounted price, the original price struck through and the discount percentage. Buyers can find them with the **Deals** filter in the Shop, including on your creator page and on your collection pages.
