# Fastlane Bowling

Study a multiplayer bowling game that simulates ball and pin physics on an authoritative server. Clients receive and replay compressed physics keyframes, ensuring every player sees the same roll. The game also persists player progression and uses multiple leaderboards and storage-backed components.

Play it in the [Fastlane World](decentraland://?realm=stom.dcl.eth).
