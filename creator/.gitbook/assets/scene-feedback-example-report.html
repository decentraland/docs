Next steps

  1. Make the start recognizable from spawn. Possibilities include a gate label or prompt, animation, directions, or moving the shop. These are candidate changes to test.
  2. Observe 3–5 first-time players. Recruit people who have not visited the scene. Measure time from arrival to starting the race.
  3. Fix the mobile feedback form before the next playtest.
  4. Try to reproduce lost coins after reconnecting.
  5. Repeat under comparable conditions: keep the same startEase question and wording, ask after the first race, and recruit players the same way. This makes a v1-to-v2 comparison more meaningful.

Scope and limitations

The analysis includes 12 players in world-v1. Each answered both questions: ease of finding the start, and an open question about the experience. The sample CSV has 31 records, or 30 unique response IDs after deduplication.

See exclusions and data checks
  • t01: the export contains a duplicate record with the same response ID; count it once.
  • debug1: a debug-mode test response.
  • preview1: a local-preview response.
  • old1: a response from world-v0. It shares question ID startEase but has different wording (“How challenging was the race?”) and a different scale (CHALLENGE). Do not combine it with the current ratings.
  • skip1: one player skipped the open question. There is no record establishing whether they saw the start question.
  • intro1 / intro2: only two participation-invitation responses are present (1 accepted, 1 declined). They cannot establish an overall participation rate.
  • This is a small sample, so counts are shown instead of percentages. Only 3 players used mobile; there is insufficient evidence for broad platform comparisons.
  • All responses share the same recorded scene time (180 seconds) and timestamp. Those fields cannot establish how long players spent looking for the start.
  • Players who answered may differ from those who did not. Proposed changes remain hypotheses. Positive comments are self-reported experiences, not measured retention or proof of overall game quality.
