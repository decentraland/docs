# Blockchain Integration

Every Decentraland account has a digital wallet behind it, even if you've never owned crypto. That wallet is what makes your Wearables, Emotes, NAMEs and LAND truly yours: their ownership is recorded on a public blockchain, not in a Decentraland database.

You don't need to understand any of this to play, or even to shop: most people buy Wearables and Emotes with [Marketplace Credits](paying-and-getting-paid.md#marketplace-credits) or a card and never deal with crypto. These pages are here for when you want to know where your things are kept, how to pay for something, or how to use the MANA you've earned.

## What You'll Learn

* [Your Account and Wallet](get-a-wallet.md): the ways to sign in, the wallet created for you when you use a social account or email, playing as a guest, and how to use your wallet outside Decentraland.
* [Paying and Getting Paid](paying-and-getting-paid.md): Marketplace Credits (earn them or buy them with a card), paying with a card or MANA, the difference between Ethereum MANA and Polygon MANA, and what to do with MANA you've earned.
* [Transactions in Polygon](transactions-in-polygon.md): gas-free Marketplace transactions and how to pay network fees with POL when you need to.
* [About the Blockchain](ethereum-essentials.md): the basic ideas behind blockchains, tokens, network fees and smart contracts.
